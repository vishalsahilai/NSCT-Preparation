# Study Strategy

For every topic:
1. Read a short explanation.
2. Watch the relevant section of a linked video when useful.
3. Write a 3–5 line summary in your own words.
4. Solve practice questions without looking at answers.
5. Explain why each wrong option is wrong.
6. Revisit missed concepts after a gap.

For programming and DSA, trace examples by hand and write small programs. For theory subjects, compare similar terms and practice scenario questions.
